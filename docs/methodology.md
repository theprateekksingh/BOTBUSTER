BOTBUSTER — Detection Methodology

Multi-Signal Risk Assessment

BOTBUSTER uses multiple account-level indicators rather than depending on one feature.

Profile Authenticity

Potential indicators include:

- Profile completeness
- Profile picture availability
- Verification status
- Account age

Engagement

Potential indicators include:

- Followers
- Following
- Likes
- Comments
- Engagement relationships

Activity Behaviour

Potential indicators include:

- Posting frequency
- Activity consistency
- Unusual activity patterns

Network Behaviour

Potential indicators include:

- Follower/following ratio
- Network characteristics
- Unusual account relationships

Risk Score

The prototype represents account risk using a 0–100 scale.

Classification

- 0–29: Low
- 30–59: Moderate
- 60–79: High
- 80–100: Critical

Explainability

The system should communicate the major signals contributing to the risk assessment so that users can understand why an account received a particular risk level.

Important Limitation

A risk score is an indicator, not definitive proof that an account is fake or malicious.
