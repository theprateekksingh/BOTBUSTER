BOTBUSTER — Project Overview

Overview

BOTBUSTER is a cybersecurity-focused AI prototype designed to assist in identifying potentially suspicious social-media accounts.

Objective

The objective is to make suspicious-account detection easier by combining multiple account-level signals into an understandable risk assessment.

Target Users

- Social-media users
- Cybersecurity analysts
- Content moderators
- Digital investigators
- Trust & safety teams
- Online communities

Core Output

BOTBUSTER produces:

- Risk score
- Risk category
- Signal breakdown
- Explanation of contributing factors
- Suggested next steps

Key Idea

Instead of relying on a single characteristic, BOTBUSTER considers multiple indicators together to provide a more useful account-level assessment.
